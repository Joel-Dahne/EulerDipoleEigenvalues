## Local changes

- Kept the interactive, human-reviewed blueprint workflow and Lean-LSP checks.
- Replaced broad campaign instructions with bounded proof, diagnosis, and review
  procedures; longer details are loaded only as needed.
- Preserved statement-first development, explicit admissions, and conditional
  assemblies without presenting them as admission-free results.
- Used the blueprint and agreed corrections as mathematical review targets,
  treating the original manuscript as evidence rather than authority.
- Omitted autonomous delegation, draft quarantine, frozen-anchor manifests,
  registry rules, module-system migration, and project-wide cleanup scripts.
- Removed imported benchmark thresholds and universal instance-cache rules;
  performance interventions require local evidence.
- Selected deterministic analysis guidance rather than probability and
  homogenization-specific recipes. No upstream code or helper scripts are
  bundled.
